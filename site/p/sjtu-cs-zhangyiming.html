个人简介（官网原文）

个人简介 Dr. Yiming Zhang's research is focused on operating, networking, storage, and AI systems. As the first/corresponding author, he has published more than 50 highly-influential papers in top conferences including SIGCOMM, NSDI, and FAST, and famous Journals like TOCS, TOS and TON. He is an associate editor of IEEE Transactions on Computers and IEEE Transactions on Services Computing, and a member of 2022 EuroSys Roger Needham Award Committee. He has received the Best Paper Award of USENIX FAST'23, the National Science and Technology Award (2nd prize), the Natural Science Award of Hunan province (1st prize), and the (top 10) Outstanding Dissertation Award.
