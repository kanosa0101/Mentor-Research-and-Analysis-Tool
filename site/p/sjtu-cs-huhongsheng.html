个人简介（官网原文）

个人简介 Dr. Hongsheng Hu is an Associate Professor in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. From 2024 to 2026, he was a Lecturer in the School of Information and Physical Sciences at the University of Newcastle, Australia. Prior to that, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at CSIRO’s Data61. He received his Ph.D. from the University of Auckland, New Zealand, under the supervision of Professor Xuyun Zhang, Professor Gillian Dobbie (Fellow of the Royal Society of New Zealand), and Professor Zoran Salcic (Fellow of the Royal Society of New Zealand). His research focuses on trustworthy machine learning, with particular emphasis on privacy and security. His work investigates critical privacy vulnerabilities in machine learning systems and develops effective defense and mitigation strategies. His research has appeared in leading information security, artificial intelligence, and data mining venues, including IEEE S&P, NDSS, USENIX Security, NeurIPS, CVPR, ICLR, IJCAI, AAAI, The Web Conference (WWW), ICDM, ACM Computing Surveys, IEEE TDSC, and IEEE TIFS. He regularly serves on the program committees of major international conferences, including ACM CCS, USENIX Security, NDSS AE, SaTML, ICLR, IJCAI, AAAI, and The Web Conference.
