个人简介（官网原文）

个人简介 李伟 ，上海交通大学计算机学院 John Hopcroft 计算机科学中心担任长聘教轨副教授 ，上海市浦江人才。他 对物理模拟领域充满热情，其研究目标是将物理学与计算机科学相结合，推动物理模拟的边界。 主要研究研究方向为计算机图形学、 物理仿真、和物理智能（为虚拟机器人创建高效的流体仿真平台，进行复杂环境下的智能学习；基于AI的物理仿真加速和优化，AI for physics等)。研究成果被发表在SIGGRAPH North America, SIGGRAPH Asia，TOG，TVCG, JCP 等国际重要会议和 期刊 上。 曾获得上海市优秀毕业生，上海科技大学校长奖等 荣誉且 多次担任SIGGRAPH North America, SIGGRAPH Asia 会议，ACM Transactions on Graphics 和IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics审稿人。 教育背景 2015–2021, ShanghaiTech University & University of Chinese Academy of Sciences Ph.D in Computer Science, Advisor: Prof. Xiaopei Liu 2011-2015, Northwestern Polytechnical University BS in Computer Science 工作履历 Associate Professor · 2025/05—now In Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, China Senior Research Scientist · 2023/02— 2025/04 In Tencent Lightspeed studio, Shanghai, China Postdoctoral Researcher · 2021/10—2023/01 In INRIA, Paris, France · Advisor: Prof. Mathieu Desbrun 论文发表 Selected publications: 请见： https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9q7M_4MAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN 个人主页： https://lwkobe.github.io/ Volume-Preserving LBM-MPM Coupling for Air-Water-Sand Mixtures, Xiaoyu Xiao, Haoxiang Wang, Xiaokang Yang, Mathieu Desbrun, and Wei Li (corresponding author) ACM Transactions on Graphics (Proceedings of SIGGRAPH 2026) Fluid Simulation with the Lattice Boltzmann Method Wei Li , Chaoyang Lyu, Mengyun Liu, Yixin Chen, Mathieu Desbrun, Kui Wu, Xiaopei Liu ACM SIGGRAPH 2026 Course Kinetic Free-Surface Flows and Foams with Sharp Interfaces Haoxiang Wang, Kui Wu, Hui Qiao, Mathieu Desbrun, Wei Li (corresponding author) ACM Transactions on Graphics (Proceedings of SIGGRAPH Asia 2025) Graphics processing unit accelerated high-order moment-encoded Lattice Boltzmann method for liquid-vapor phase-change simulations Wei Li, Linlin Fei, and Ya-Ling He,  accepted by Physics of Fluids, 2025.
