个人简介 Personal Profile [Personal Homepage] (for more information) [ Academic family tree ] Shixiong Zhang is an Associate Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Xidian University, starting in December 2020. He spent three years (2017-2020) obtaining his Ph.D. in Computer Science under the mentorship of Dr. Ka-Chun Wong from the City University of Hong Kong . He was previously a research trainee in the Lab of Prof. Jiang Qian at the Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine . His broad research interests include bioinformatics, computational biology, machine learning, and computational intelligence.
