个人简介（官网原文）

个人简介 Erci Xu is a tenure-track associate professor @ Shanghai Jiao Tong University who mostly focuses on cloud, storage systems, reliability and infrastructure for AI. He has published multiple papers at top venues including SOSP, OSDI, FAST, ATC and Eurosys. He is the recipient of two USENIX FAST Best Paper Awards FAST'23, FAST'24, Distinguished Artifacts Award from FAST'25, ACM SIGOPS China Rising Star Award and 2025 CCF-IST Youth Innovation Award. In his spare time, Erci enjoys reading and reviewing books. This website is not actively maintained.
