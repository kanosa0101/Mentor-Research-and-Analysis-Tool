SIGCOMM China 新星奖、江苏省计算机学会优秀科技工作者、江苏省计算机学会青年科技奖、南京大学青年五四奖章、第八届中国国际“互联网＋”全国银奖、第十八届“挑战杯”全国大学生课外学术科技作品竞赛主体赛一等奖、全国高校物联网创新大赛一等奖及最佳指导教师奖等荣誉称号。主要研究方向为物联网、边缘智能、移动计算、数据挖掘等。发表和接收论文 300 余篇（含 CCF A 类 130 余篇），包括 http://cs.nju.edu.cn/daihp 电子邮件： haipengdai@nju.edu.cn Haipeng Dai is a professor, doctoral supervisor with the School of Computer Science at Nanjing University, Young Chang Jiang Scholar, and IET Fellow. He received the B.S. degree in the Department of Electronic Engineering in Shanghai Jiaotong University in 2007, the M.S. degree in Department of Electrical Engineering in Shanghai Jiaotong University in 2010, and the Ph.D. degree in the Department of Computer Science and Technology in Nanjing University in 2014. He received ACM China Rising Star Award in 2018 (2 awardees in China), IEEE TCSC Middle Career Researcher Award (4 awardees worldwide), Excellent Scientific and Technological Member of Chinese Institute of Electronics, ACM SIGCOMM China Rising Star Award, Excellent Scientific and Technological Member of Jiangsu Computer Society, Youth Science and Technology Award of Jiangsu Computer Society, Excellent Doctoral Dissertation of Jiangsu Computer Society, Youth May-4th Medal of Nanjing University, Silver Award of the 8th China International College Students’ Internet+ Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition , and First Prize and Best Instructor Award of National College Competition on Internet of Things. His research interests are mainly in the areas of Internet of Things, edge intelligence, data mining, and mobile computing. He has published more than 300 papers (including 130 CCF A papers), including ACM UbiComp, ACM EuroSys, USENIX ATC, IEEE INFOCOM, VLDB, IEEE ICDE, ACM WWW, ACM SIGMETRICS, ACM MobiHoc, ACM MobiSys, IEEE ICNP, IEEE ICDCS, ACM/IEEE TON, IEEE JSAC, IEEE TPDS, IEEE TMC, The VLDB Journal, IEEE TKDE, IEEE TDSC, IEEE TIFS, and other top international conference journals. He received Best Paper Award Candidate from CCF A conference IEEE INFOCOM, Best Paper Award from CCF B conference IEEE ICNP, Best Paper Award Runner-up from CCF B conference IEEE SECON, Best Paper Award from CCF C conference IEEE HPCC, Best Paper Award from CCF C conference IEEE WASA, Best Paper Award from CCF C conference APWeb-WAIM, Best Paper Award from IEEE Cybertics Congress 2025 (including CCF C conference ICA3PP), and other paper awards. He has 6 papers selected as ESI highly cited papers and 1 paper selected as ESI hot paper. His Google Scholar citation has reached over 6200 times, with citations from more than 100 academicians ACM/IEEE Fellow. He chaired and participated in more than ten projects including Key Projects of Joint Funds of NSFC, General Projects of NSFC and the National Key R&D Program of China, and the relevant research results have been implemented in Huawei, Alibaba, Tencent, ZTE, Sinopec, and other companies. He was selected for Stanford University's Top 2% Scientists list. He serves as the distinguished member of CCF, the senior member of ACM and IEEE, secretary general of ACM SIGCOMM China, chair of CCF YOCSEF Nanjing, vice director of Jiangsu Computer Society Youth Working Committee, vice director of JiangSu Association of Artificial lntelligence Intelligence and Security Committee, member of CCF Technical Committee of Internet of Things, CCF Technical Committee of Network and Data Communication, and CCF Technical Committee of Database, member of CIC Technical Committee of Cloud Computing and Big Data Applications, and CIC Technical Committee of Open Source, young member of CIE Technical Committee of Internet of Things, member of ACM SIGBED China and ACM SIGAPP China. He is the program committee chair of CCF A conference INFOCOM Workshop WNA 2022, publication chair of CCF B conference ICNP 2014, tutorial & workshop chair of CCF B conference COCOON 2024, TPC chair of CCF C conference ISPA 2022, TPC vice-chair of CCF C conference HPCC 2021, track chair of CCF C conferences ICCCN 2019 and ICPADS 2021, workshop chair of CCF C conference MSN 2023, TPC chair of EI Conference iThings 2021, and other positions. He also serves as the program committee member of important international academic conferences such as INFOCOM, IJCAI, VLDB, SIGKDD, SC, MobiHoc, ICNP, ICDCS, IWQoS, and IPDPS. In addition, he serves as an associate editor of Chinese CCF A Journal Acta Electronica Sinica, associate editor of TII, associate editor of Chinese CCF B Journal Computer Science, and guest editor of CCF B Journal JSA. He has served as a reviewer for more than 50 international top journals such as TON, JSAC, TPDS, TMC, and TKDE. He has guided several students to win the President's Special Scholarship, ICNP Best Paper Award, SECON Best Paper Award runner-up, Silver Award of the 8th China International College Students’ Internet+ Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition, and First Prize of National College Competition on Internet of Things, etc. Some of his students have been employed by head companies such as Tencent, Ali, Huawei, ByteDance, Ant Financial, Microsoft, Netease, and Huatai Securities. Some have joined Tsinghua University, Nanjing University, National University of Defense Technology, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, and others are studying at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, Purdue University, Rice University, and University of Minnesota.
