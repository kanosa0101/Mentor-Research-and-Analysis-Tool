个人简介（官网原文）

个人简介 I am currently a professor in the School of Computer Science @ Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I was supported by NSFC Excellent Young Scholars Program.  I received my Ph.D. from the CS department @ Shanghai Jiao Tong University in March 2018. My advisors were Prof. Prof. Fan Wu and Prof. Guihai Chen. I was a visiting research scholar and then a postdoctoral researcher in the Coordinated Science Laboratory (CSL) @ UIUC from September 2016 to October 2019, advised by Prof. R. Srikant. I am the recipient of the China Computer Federation (CCF) Excellent Doctoral Dissertation Award in 2018. During my Ph.D. program, I was supported by Google Ph.D. Fellowship and Microsoft Research Asia Ph.D. Fellowship. My research interests are in the intersection of economics, learning and system.
